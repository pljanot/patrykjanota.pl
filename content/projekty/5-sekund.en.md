##### 5 seconds (2023)

Building a meaningful relationship with another person is a treasure that, in my view, we as people have stopped valuing. The apparent ease of making fleeting, meaningless acquaintances fills the Internet to the brim. This series of photographs presents my point of view on **human relationships in the 21st century**. The title refers directly to the exposure time of a single sheet of film.

Each work was made by hand and is unique; it cannot be repeated because of the **partial development** technique, in which the chemistry is applied to the paper unevenly.

The project concluded with a solo exhibition held on **16.06.23 – Gdańsk – Mikstura Space**.
