##### Blank Sheet (2021 – 2022)

**"Inversion"** – Using a large format camera, it shows the calm of everyday life interrupted by the events of war. The symbolic transformation of a positive into a negative is a response to the emotional state I found myself in. I put emphasis on the materiality of my works, which are contact prints and correspond 1:1 to reality.
