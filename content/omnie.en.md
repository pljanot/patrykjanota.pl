##### About:

A programmer fascinated by analogue photography. In his projects he uses small, medium and large format cameras. He is interested in unpredictability. He makes the enlargements of his works himself.

Graduate of Szkoła Karola, the photography school run by Karol Krukowski. Participant of a group exhibition at the International Culture Laboratory in Sokołowsko. Repeatedly distinguished in photography competitions, including Fotoglob, Moje Małe i Duże Mikro Podróże and Otwarte Mistrzostwa Fotograficzne. For 12 years the owner of a mongrel named Nuta.
